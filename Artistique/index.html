✳ GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might wonder.

Have a different question? Just ask me.

How far in advance should we book? +

Most couples reach out 9–15 months ahead, especially for summer Saturdays. That said, lovely things happen last minute too—ask me about your date.

Do you travel for events? +

Absolutely. I’m based in Portland and photograph all over the Pacific Northwest, with a few passport stamps every year. Tell me where you’re dreaming of and we’ll make a plan.

What’s your cancellation policy? +

Your retainer reserves the date and is non-refundable. If plans change, I’ll always do my best to transfer your booking or help find a solution that feels fair.

How long until we receive our photos? +

You’ll get a little preview within a week. Your full, hand-edited gallery arrives in 6–8 weeks, ready to download, share, and revisit as often as you like.

Do you offer raw files? +

I don’t deliver raw files. Each photograph is carefully selected and edited to make a cohesive gallery that feels true to your day and to my work.
