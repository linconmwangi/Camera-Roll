✳ A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I am Alex Murage.
I notice the little things others overlook.

The shaky laugh before the vows. Your dad trying not to cry. The friends who never left the dance floor. Those are the moments I am here for.

For the past decade, I have photographed love stories with a documentary heart and an editorial eye. I will give you room to be yourselves, and gentle direction when you need it. The result? Photographs that feel a little like you.

✳ GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might wonder.

Have a different question? Just ask me.

How far in advance should we book? +

Most couples reach out 9–15 months ahead, especially for summer Saturdays. That said, lovely things happen last minute too ask me about your date.

Do you travel for events? +

Absolutely. I am based in Rongai, Kenya and photograph across Kenya, with a few passport stamps every year. Tell me where you are dreaming of and we will make a plan.

What is your cancellation policy? +

Your retainer reserves the date and is non-refundable. If plans change, I will always do my best to transfer your booking or help find a solution that feels fair.

How long until we receive our photos? +

You will get a little preview within a week. Your full, hand-edited gallery arrives in 6–8 weeks, ready to download, share, and revisit as often as you like.

Do you offer raw files? +

I do not deliver raw files. Each photograph is carefully selected and edited to make a cohesive gallery that feels true to your day and to my work.
